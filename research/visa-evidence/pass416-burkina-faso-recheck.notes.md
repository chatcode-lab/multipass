# Pass 416 — US → Burkina Faso

Researcher: root. Reopened 29 September 2026; independent review required before publication.

The government's signed 30 December 2025 communiqué was visually read, including signature/stamp, from the image linked by [its government article](https://gouvernement.gov.bf/?p=9797). The article's text is mostly navigation; the actual image matters. It announces reciprocal visa measures, not a precisely scoped ordinary-visitor entry ban. The linked PDF failed in the browser tool, but the government image downloaded and rendered successfully.

The current [US travel guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/burkina-faso.html) was directly readable in the browser tool (the native request returned a challenge). Both the overview and entry section state suspended visa issuance with limited exceptions. Neither describes already-issued visas or the exception categories. The US security advice not to travel is not a Burkina Faso prohibition on entry.

The government-domain Washington [visa page](https://etats-unis.diplomatie.gov.bf/index.php/visa/) and [embassy website](https://burkina-usa.org/), linked from State's page, retain generic online-visa links. This is not evidence the US suspension was lifted. Bounded searches for embassy clarification found no directly retrievable original notice resolving the exception/valid-visa questions. A secondary report about a January embassy communiqué is discovery only and is not retained as evidence.

Outcome: no replacement status, no new exact/conditional coverage, no permitted-stay number. A renewed, independently reviewed correction explanation may cite these two new source records through 29 October; this is a research recheck date, not a legal expiry. Do not overwrite the August source records or imply that every entry by a US citizen is banned.

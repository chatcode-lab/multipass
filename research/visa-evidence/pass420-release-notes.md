All three candidates passed exact validation and independent source review. Twelve source records and three policies were appended. The earlier artifacts remain unchanged. The two Solomon Islands routes do not receive a numerical stay or invented commencement date. The two other routes add stay coverage and one point each relative to the bundled normalized baseline. Relative to the public snapshot checked in this pass only Israel gains a point: Colombia's imported visa-free label already scored the same as ETA. A before/after calculation on the same normalized bundled input produced identical combination research results, so the dated analysis artifact was not regenerated or restamped.

The ±30-day expiry scan found only the already handled 31 October Montenegro and Russia changes. The current Chad immigration portal says visitors without an exemption can apply online, but this pass did not establish Palestinian ordinary-document eligibility and pre-travel electronic issuance together. Keep the other remaining discrepancies (Albania → Morocco, Nigeria → Tanzania and Palestine → Chad) and Cambodia's post-trial successor open; do not renew their verification dates. No raw snapshot, applicant records, credentials, cache configuration or country-profile data were changed.

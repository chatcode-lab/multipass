## Schedule reconciliation and exclusions

The Schedule contains numbering and naming quirks that must not create extra catalog relationships:

- It skips number 59; numbering is not a reliable count.
- It lists both Côte d'Ivoire and Ivory Coast; both are one CI nationality.
- Its northern-part-of-Cyprus entry does not describe the Republic of Cyprus ordinary passport (CY), and no northern-Cyprus passport exists in the catalog.
- Its Colombia spelling is a longstanding typo. The existing mapping is corroborated by the readable July 2025 official schedule; no distinct country is introduced.
- The announcement's Nauru misspelling is resolved by the operative Schedule's explicit Nauru entry.
- The former Taiwan identity-number exception does not control current Bermuda entry because Taiwan is removed altogether, rather than retained with an asterisk. Do not borrow the UK's continued Taiwan entry back into Bermuda.

The 75 controlled codes were transcribed against the new Schedule and reconciled with the catalog. The 122 unlisted ordinary-passport codes plus HK/MO exhaust the remaining catalog after applying the positive statutory baseline. No new citizenship, diplomatic, refugee, transit, work, residence or school-party claim is made.

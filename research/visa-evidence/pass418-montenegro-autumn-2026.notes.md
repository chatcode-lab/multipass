# Pass 418: Montenegro autumn visa-rule freshness

Researcher: `/root/pass418_montenegro`. Actual source reads: 20 October 2026. Scope is exactly KZ, BY, RU, SA and TR ordinary visitor passports into ME. This is a research candidate, not self-approval or permission to publish.

## Result and enrichment

The existing category boundaries are reconfirmed: KZ requires an advance visa from 2 October 2026; BY/RU and SA/TR retain their 30-day ordinary-passport waivers through 31 October and require advance visas from 1 November. No extension or new electronic issuance route was established. Current nationality pages agree with the consolidated regulation and the explicit July MFA announcement. The September Government follow-up provides additional current evidence for the November transition.

Four same-scope refresh records supply fresh 20 October source reads, the four current 30-day stay allowances, and a qualified short-stay visa C ceiling for KZ now and the four successor nationalities from November. The latter is at most 90 days in 180 days counted from first entry, subject to the visa actually granted. It is not a claim that every visa permits 90 days, or that visa validity is 90 days. At the research date this should add five current structured-stay relationships, with no category or score change. November successors preserve those four relationships' structured stay after their category transition.

The exact candidate validates: nine sources, four policies, five explicitly explained upstream/prospective conflicts, no conditional record, no unresolved relationship. KZ's bundled upstream visa-free value differs from the current already-corrected live result. BY/RU/SA/TR conflict rows are expressly prospective, not a present-day correction. Independent review requested that the C-visa stay basis preserve the stated first-entry anchor rather than imply a sliding window. Both C-visa records therefore use `calendar_period`, expressly label the first-entry anchor, and retain the 90/180 maximum and issued-visa qualification.

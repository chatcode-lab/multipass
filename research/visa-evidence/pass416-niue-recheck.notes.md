## Reopened guidance and reconsidered interpretation

The [live Tourism entry page](https://www.niueisland.com/discover-niue/travelling-to-niue) still has the detailed nationality list at browser lines 82-90 and an explicitly New Zealand-inclusive FAQ at 203-204. The detailed list describes an arrival visitor permit but omits New Zealand, then supplies a general advance-visa instruction. The FAQ describes a short tourist visa waiver for New Zealand without explaining stay-permit issuance. Both passages remain accessible together.

The earlier research log's assertion that both passages affirmatively contradict the imported arrival label is not established. Omission is not positive nationality evidence, and an exemption from a travel visa can coexist with an arrival-issued stay permit. The page's ancestry and residence stickers also should not be transformed automatically into multiple normalized tourist statuses: some notes concern longer residence or return travel, not the ordinary short-tourist default.

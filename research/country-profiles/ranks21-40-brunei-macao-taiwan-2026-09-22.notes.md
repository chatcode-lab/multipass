### Brunei citizenship

- The [current AGC register](https://www.agc.gov.bn/documents/brunei-nationality-act/) was read by direct public HTTP after the web reader failed. The current BRULAW B index identifies the Act as in force. The statute record lists the 2011 edition as latest and the 2002 subsidiary regulations; the migration's 2026 upload paths do not mean these are 2026 enactments. Old AGC ASPX/cap015 paths returned 404 and are not retained.
- The [2011 Nationality Act](https://www.agc.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/cap-15-2011-ed-brunei-nationality-act.pdf) was downloaded successfully and all 25 PDF pages read using PDFKit. Printed pages are two below the PDF page number. Section 2(2) supplies age/capacity; section 8 supplies twenty within twenty-five, the final two years, character, support, language, intention, counting and oath. Section 9 distinguishes later nationality acquisition and long absence; section 11 addresses deprivation; section 13 addresses reasons and review. The Second Schedule is an allegiance/nonexercise oath, not an express requirement in this text to present a foreign government's renunciation certificate before application.
- The [Nationality Registration Regulations](https://www.agc.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/cap-15-rg-1-brunei-nationality-registration-regulations.pdf) were downloaded and the operative regulations and relevant Form E1 material read. Regulation 8 uses E1/E2. Regulation 9(3) gives practical language proficiency. Regulation 16 is unnumbered within the regulation and provides Form K; the final locator check removed a draft's erroneous `16(3)` reference.
- Section 8(3)'s consent exception is attached to temporary-pass/permit residence, not unlawful residence or custody. Independent review corrected the decision-maker from Minister to His Majesty: the printed text refers to His Majesty in Council, with the S 29/1995 footnote transferring that power to His Majesty. Section 8(2)'s absence credits do not authorise counting every trip abroad.
- Immigration's [live Malay citizenship page](https://www.immigration.gov.bn/warganegara/) was additionally read. It has clear copy/paste inconsistencies in its section 8 subsection (the heading refers to birth abroad but one bullet refers to birth inside, and an attention note mixes B1/5(1) with the section 8/E1 route). It mentions written testing with particular oral alternatives. It is therefore not used to replace the clean statutory baseline or to assert that every applicant takes only an oral test. Operational testing, documents and current forms must be checked with Immigration. The candidate makes no test-format guarantee.

### Macao taxes

- [Law 24/2024 and the Tax Code](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2024/53/lei24.asp) were read from actual direct-fetch Portuguese HTML. Approving-law Article 29(1),(3) gives the general 1 January 2026 commencement but expressly starts Code Article 24 on 1 January 2025. Independent review corrected the locator from paragraphs 1–2 to paragraphs 1 and 3; the stated commencement dates did not change.
- Code Article 24: at least 183 continuous/intermittent days in the calendar income year, or a 31 December home indicating intention to keep and occupy habitual residence; any full or partial day counts. It is not simply any address at year-end. Article 22: representative duty applies to natural-person taxpayers abroad and qualifying absentees, with electronic-notification election exception. These are distinct tests.
- Code Article 14 establishes territorial application subject to agreements/other law. The official [DSF infographic](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/uploads/dsf/20250328/1.jpg) was downloaded and visually inspected. It was reached from the actual DSF [Tax Code infographic directory](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/topic/taxlaw/pt/taxlawgraphic). The multinational-entity foreign-passive-income exception is not assigned to all individual residents. The separate DSF residence infographic was also visually checked as corroboration but need not be a duplicate retained source.
- The [DSF professional-tax page](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/pt/tax/tax_introduction/salaries_tax) was read in full: Macao work irrespective of payment origin/place/currency; employed group and specified independent liberal/technical professions; employer registration/withholding/remittance. The candidate avoids calling every self-employed business a listed profession.
- The [DSF treaty page](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/en/taxtax_avoiddoubletax) was actually read for residence declarations and mutual-agreement procedures. The Portuguese statute, not a shortened English residence summary, controls the home/intention wording.

### Taiwan nationality

- The [Chinese Nationality Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0030001) was actually read in full. Its latest amendment is 24 May 2024 and the law database displayed a current 11 September 2026 cutoff. The [English translation](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0030001) was also read as an access/translation comparison but is not retained as the operative authority.
- Article 3 uses an inclusive minimum of 183 days in each of five consecutive years, not the English translation's misleading `more than` formulation. Article 9 concerns settlement while nationality-loss proof is outstanding, not a prohibition on all ordinary residence. The candidate relies on the Chinese distinctions.
- Articles 3-6 distinguish the ordinary route, family connections, specially recommended/reviewed high-level professionals and special contributions. Articles 8-9 distinguish permission, original-nationality proof, timing/extensions/exceptions and settlement. A reduced route is not promised to anyone merely holding a work permit.
- The [MOI language/civics FAQ Q2](https://www.ris.gov.tw/documents/html/8/7/2/144_Q02.html) was actually read. It provides alternative domestic schooling, government courses or a test. Ordinary applicants: 200 course hours or a 70/100 test, not the 60-point score used for some special cohorts. Age-65 and other reduced cohorts remain separately qualified. No CEFR mapping is invented.
- [NIA Guidelines 0305](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/141950/) were actually read in the earlier successful web response; a later repeat fetch timed out. The content was dated 10 April 2024. Applicable section II(II) covers naturalised people with residency permission, VII(I) describes later residence alternatives, and VI(III) directs household registration after the settlement certificate. The candidate deliberately does not republish the entire numerical settlement regime as if it were the initial naturalisation rule.

- B1 official description page 1 explicitly identifies CEFR/MECR B1 and the five assessed skill groups. No exam timing/points are transcribed from its table. No lower-level conversion or automatic integration exemption inferred.
- Actual May 1996 integration regulation was also inspected, but not used to impose its old A-level certificate/exemption scheme after the 2026 amendment. The current application links a 2019 amendment too. Candidate sticks to current statutory integration scope and explicitly limits detailed exemptions. Law 8/2026 final provision one allows old regulations only insofar as not conflicting pending updated rules.
- Read actual current IRPF Articles 2, 4, 5, 8 and 48 and actual government residence FAQ. More than 183 is strict, not at least 183; the economic centre is an alternative test; family presumption joins spouse AND minor children. Foreign-tax credit has actual-paid, similar-tax, domestic-cap and treaty qualifications.
- Read actual current IRNR Articles 1, 3, 4 and 8 supporting nonresident source scope; do not generalise rates or assume every receipt is taxed.

## Deliberate boundaries

- No typed residence constraint for the nationality period: the statute's principal/permanent and recent principal/effective residence should not silently become a PR-permit holding period or a physical-presence threshold in the current schema.
- Ordinary route is Article 11(1). Separate school-based, marriage and ancestry grounds are not substituted for it.
- Article 18's voluntary acquisition and Article 23's involuntary nationality/active exercise were read. They require final administrative/judicial treatment and do not justify a general incoming-naturalisation renunciation waiver. Those detailed loss rules are not duplicated in the page's baseline.
- Avoid the original law's historical transitional five-year window and avoid confusing it with the present Article 28 post-conditional-decision deadline. Current extension is up to two years upon proof the renunciation procedure began.
- The general IRPF service page was read but its older real-estate-gain description was not relied on. Current law supplies income scope and the narrow tax facts here; property-transfer rules and rates remain outside scope.

## Review cautions and unresolved boundaries

- Denmark's processing notice is central, not a footer: eligibility summaries do not promise that ordinary applications are currently being processed. No replacement post-election conditions were established in the read authorities.
- Denmark's native permanent-status page measures the holding period by the end of the expected adoption month; the English overview compresses this to the time the Act is passed. Candidate preserves the more precise native wording.
- Denmark's native Test 2 page includes both the two-year no-assistance condition and the nine-year three-month limit. The English overview abbreviates the former; candidate uses the native page.
- Danish test names are retained without inferred CEFR equivalence. The 2015 civic-test concession is limited to the June 2016 through June 2021 sittings. Disability is a route to parliamentary waiver consideration, not automatic waiver.
- The Danish overview and native employment page were both read. Their detailed hour formulations differ; the candidate states only the shared residence-independent three-and-a-half-within-four-year requirement and current labour-market condition, with exceptions. A full employment calculation is outside scope.
- Danish citizenship guidance distinguishes Greenland/Faroe situations. The candidate does not extend the Denmark-resident dual-nationality or ceremony summary to those territories. A linked historical naturalisation circular PDF was opened, but its text search was unreliable; no candidate claim depends on that extraction.
- Norwegian A2 concessions and full exemptions remain distinct. No fixed currency amount is given for sufficient income because the basic amount and usable tax assessment vary. The citizenship concession does not waive permanent-residence self-support conditions.
- Norwegian tax-emigration guidance uses a heading of ten years or more but says more than ten in one body sentence. The earlier combined official tax-residence page explicitly states ten or more. The candidate retains the inclusive boundary and the dedicated page's definition of prior tax-resident years, measured through the year before departure.
- Danish six-month guidance is the displayed 2026-1 edition, cross-checked against the live general taxpayer page. The short baseline does not claim review of every later judicial decision or every special tourist/student case. The legal-guide text establishes at least six months; the general English page alternates six months and exceeds six months, so the candidate uses the technical guidance.
- Neither tax topic covers personal rates, special inbound schemes, complete wealth/exit-tax calculations, offshore employment, Svalbard, Greenland or Faroe Islands rules. Treaty outcomes require the actual bilateral instrument and facts.

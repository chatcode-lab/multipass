## Vatican City / Holy See: four sources

Visually read Law CXXXI's office/service, authorised family, loss, residence and commencement provisions on scanned pages 2–5 and 7, with the signed enactment. Citizenship is not a normal residence-duration naturalisation route. Residence permission is not citizenship; office/service and dependent-family conditions matter. Temporary absence preserving a home and the child's age-eighteen rule are qualified. The law takes effect 1 March 2011.

Read the actual Lateran Treaty Articles 9 and 17. The Italian-nationality fallback is conditional on not retaining another nationality under Italian law. The remuneration exemption applies in Italy to specified payors, recipients and remuneration, not all income of all Vatican citizens.

Read the 2015 tax convention's Articles 1–2 and 5 in the actual PDF, including pages 2–7 and 11–12, and the 14 October 2016 entry-into-force announcement. Existing Italian tax residence and the defined clerical/employment/pension cohorts are prerequisites. The financial-income/intermediary mechanism excludes the stated nonfinancial categories. Historical entry windows are not advertised as currently open. Information exchange and confidentiality do not establish universal automatic exchange or tax exemption. The absence of a collected comprehensive Vatican domestic individual-tax baseline remains an explicit unresolved fact, not a zero-tax claim.

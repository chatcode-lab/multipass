## Vatican City / Holy See / Italy

- Law CXXXI, 22 February 2011, effective 1 March 2011, is the actual eight-page official scan. Articles 1-3 establish institutional, request-based and dependent-family citizenship; Article 6 distinguishes residence without citizenship. The topic is `exceptional`, with no invented years or language threshold.
- Current Governorate population guidance was also actually read: `https://www.vaticanstate.va/fr/etat-et-gouvernorat/notes-generales/population.html`. It distinguishes citizens living outside the State from residents who are not citizens; its statistics are expressly at 31 December 2024 and are not republished in this legal packet. The current legislation listing was located; no later citizenship amendment was found in the official search. The original law is not mislabelled as a 2026 enactment.
- Read Lateran Treaty Articles 9 and 17 in the Holy See's full official text. Article 9 is a conditional nationality fallback, not a tax-residence rule or automatic Italian citizenship for all former Vatican citizens. Article 17 applies in Italian territory to specified remuneration/payors/recipients; not foreign investment income generally.
- Read the full 2015 tax convention's operative relevant clauses: Article 1 information exchange; Article 2 eligibility and financial-income arrangements; Article 5 excluded income classes and preservation of Article 17; Articles 8-9 and 13-14 for context. Read the 14 October 2016 official announcement confirming entry into force on 15 October 2016. Do not present the historical initial election window as currently open.
- Four positive tax facts are established within this narrow bilateral scope. A fifth explicitly records that a general Vatican domestic personal-tax residence/foreign-income regime was not established. No “tax-free country”, universal Italian tax residence, worldwide exemption or zero rate is claimed. A 2015 official commentary describing the Vatican's then tax arrangements was not used as a comprehensive current 2026 domestic-law source.

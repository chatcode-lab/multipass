## Saint Kitts and Nevis

- Read the 2020 Citizenship Act revision, including sections 2, 6 and 7, the Second Schedule and regulations 4–6. The ordinary alien route, capacity, residence counting, discretion, forms and oath are supported. The unusual Second Schedule wording gives a fourteen-year earlier window without an aggregate-duration numeral; that uncertainty is preserved, not silently repaired.
- Read Constitution sections 92–95. Section 93's foreign-nationality retention protection is tied to the section 92 registration cohort, not every ordinary naturalisation applicant. No language exemption or universal dual-nationality conclusion is inferred.
- Read Income Tax Act section 3(3), the whole relevant section 36 and section 90 in the 2017 revision. The general charge excludes individuals, but covered nonresident withholding explicitly includes them. Independently fetched and read the IRD corporate-tax page's opening historical explanation confirming abolition for individuals; its corporate residence rules were not imported.
- Read all seven pages of the Unincorporated Business Tax Act: business/employment boundaries, gross-income calculation, the continuing election and quarter-end filing language. Old rates and thresholds are omitted.
- Also actually read all three pages of the September 2026 descendant-registration bill. Its enactment fields are blank and commencement depends on an order. It is not adopted as an operative ordinary-naturalisation amendment.

## Antigua and Barbuda

- Read the base Citizenship Act sections 2, 6 and 7 and Second/Third Schedules together with all five pages of Act 5 of 2021. The final residence year, preceding five-in-seven calculation, ministerial discretion, character, intention, oath and limited counting rules match. Requested correction: the 2021 replacement is amendment section 2, not section 3. A shorter unlawful stay is not automatically credited.
- Read Constitution sections 114–115. The independence-era date/cohort limitation on express dual-nationality protection remains visible. The inaccessible immigration FAQ is not cited; its possible operational language/interview and retention details remain unresolved.
- Read the 2016 business-tax Act's definitions and operative sections 3–4, 9–13 and 16–17 in its booklet-ordered PDF. Employment is excluded; covered business/rental income is separate. Section 3's foreign-income scope is qualified by section 4's no-incidental-local-activity/source-jurisdiction-tax-liability exemption and separate temporary-visitor rule. A general “tax-free passport” or unconditional territorial claim would not follow.
- Read all four pages of the IRD FAQ and the incorporated Income Tax Act residence definition on printed pages 6–7. Nonresident property-agent and withholding duties, quarterly filing, annual 31 March filing and agreement-specific exemptions are supported. The older Income Tax Act is used only for the expressly incorporated definition, not to revive abolished personal income tax.
- Also actually read all five pages of the 2019 business-tax amendment: it adds deemed registration, not a new residence or income-scope test.

## Material limits for independent review

1. Slovak inbound former-nationality retention remains explicitly `not_established`. The full ordinary statutory conditions were read, but no express primary authority statement establishing universal renunciation or retention was found. The independently documented outbound loss rules must not be mistaken for an inbound renunciation rule.
2. Czech Russian-nationality processing is documented, but the candidate expressly leaves the separate section 7x nationality-loss requirements for additional confirmation. e-Sbírka returned no operative text. An actual court-hosted senators' petition in case Pl. ÚS 15/25 was opened and its reproduction of enacted sections 7x/7y read, but it is a litigation submission, not an authoritative current consolidation or a judgment, so it is not used as the candidate's legal authority. General dual-nationality permission is qualified rather than applied to Russian nationals. No claim is made that a challenge has repealed the rules.
3. Lithuania's current citizenship consolidation retains older disability/working-capacity terminology. The candidate therefore states the statutory exemption categories without translating them into a new participation-percentage framework. The numerical age exemption is established; no CEFR level is guessed.
4. Some Slovak Financial Administration summaries still include a cross-border commuter exclusion. Current section 2(e) includes treaty residence and sole study/treatment, not that generic commuter exclusion. The candidate follows the actual current law and flags the conflict.
5. Lithuania's special day-count exclusions, including temporary-protection situations, are not comprehensively established by this baseline. The ordinary day tests are conditional and the limitations preserve that boundary. No temporary-protection tax exemption is inferred.

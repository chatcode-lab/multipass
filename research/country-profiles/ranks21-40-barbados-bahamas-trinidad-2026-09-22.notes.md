## Barbados

- The Judiciary's Cap. 186 PDF was read at sections 2, 4, 7-8 and the Second Schedule. It has older revision dates, not a 2026 consolidation. The alien route is a final twelve months plus five aggregate years in the preceding seven. Commonwealth/Irish registration and British-protected-person naturalisation have different seven-year/five-year conditions. Section 2's absence credits and excluded periods remain material.
- The Immigration Department's [live downloads page](https://immigration.gov.bb/pages/Downloads.aspx) links the retained N.1 form. All four scanned PDF pages were rendered and visually read. Page 2 distinguishes the alien and British-protected cohorts by footnotes; pages 3-4 contain declarations, references and notice/submission instructions. The old printed fee and monarch-specific oath were not republished as current.
- [Bill 912](https://www.barbadosparliament.com/bills/details/912) identifies the Barbados Citizenship Bill, 2026 with notice dated 21 April 2026 and the House of Assembly as its current stage. The [Parliament homepage](https://www.barbadosparliament.com/) still places it among bills before the House and links a joint-select-committee report. Neither a bill formatted as an Act nor its proposal text was substituted for enacted law.
- [FCDO living-in guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/living-in-barbados), Citizenship subsection, expressly confirms British citizens need not renounce UK nationality to become Barbadian. This is official guidance from the original nationality's authority; the candidate does not turn it into a guarantee for every foreign nationality.
- BRA's live Individuals page confirms the residence/domicile/foreign-benefit distinction. The linked Cap. 73 PDF was checked at sections 16-17 and 85(5)-(7). The ordinary-residence definition and written non-resident election qualify the short BRA summary. A foreign benefit is broader than a cash remittance.
- The [BRA income-tax legislation index](https://www.bra.gov.bb/About/Legislation-Regulations/Income-Tax-Act) was read. Its [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2025-30](https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments/Income+Tax+(Amendment)+Act,+2025-30.pdf&name=Income+Tax+(Amendment)+Act,+2025-30) was read in full: nine PDF pages about corporate economic-substance reporting, not changes to the cited personal residence/income provisions. ## The Bahamas

- Read the official principal Nationality Act, especially sections 2-3, 5, 9 and the complete Second Schedule. The preceding-nine-years requirement is six aggregate qualifying years plus the final year, not ten full years of residence. Good character, English/civic knowledge, permanent-home intention and section 9's renunciation/declaration alternatives remain in the candidate.
- The official [legislation alphabetical index](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/legislation/index/by-alphabetical.html) and [Acts by year](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/legislation/acts_only/acts-by-year.html) identify the 2026-0032 amendment. The latter's actual HTML links the retained `2026-0032A/2026-0032A.pdf` (the guessed path without `A` returned 404 and is not cited). All three PDF pages were read. It was assented to on 30 June and commenced 1 July 2026; it inserts section 7A, concerning discretionary permanent residence after section 7 refusal, without replacing ordinary naturalisation section 9 or the Second Schedule.
- Form 6's actual first-page supporting-document checklist was read. Later scanned application pages were not relied on for additional claims. Its 2019 upload path and legacy fee/oath limitations are disclosed; neither a current fee nor an invented CEFR standard is published.

## Trinidad and Tobago

- The [official legislative-library index](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/list?currentid=538&offset=0&q=s) identifies the retained Ch. 1:50 file and the later regulations amendment. Web-reader access to the Act failed, but unauthenticated Node HTTPS retrieval returned the 60-page PDF, and native PDFKit extracted the actual sections. The cover says unofficial version updated to 31 December 2016; this edition limitation is stated rather than relabelled 2026.
- Read sections 2, 7, 11-13A and the recorded 2000 amendments. The alien route's final twelve months plus five of the prior seven differs from Commonwealth/Irish/British-protected registration's five continuous years. Deleted intention-to-reside and prior-renunciation text was not resurrected. Section 11's protection against later citizenship acquisition causing loss is not generalised from birth/descent citizens to all naturalised persons.
- The retained March 2026 checklist was read in full. It requests permanent-resident evidence **or immigration-status evidence**, not a newly invented universal PR-duration rule. The [ministry's naturalised-citizenship landing page](https://homelandsecurity.gov.tt/appforms/naturalised-citizenship/) mislabels section 12(1) as an Immigration Act provision. The actual citizenship statute, not that landing-page citation error, controls the candidate's attribution.
- Read the four-page [Citizenship Regulations amendment, Legal Notice 490 of 2025](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/123444?type=amendment), downloaded directly from the same official library: effective 1 January 2026, it replaces fee entries. It does not replace the statutory eligibility rules. Fee-table cohort wording was not used to invent a new naturalisation category.
- The IRD [legislation index](https://www.ird.gov.tt/law-policy/legislation) links the retained 2024 Act PDF. Sections 5(1)-(2), 47 and 76(4) were actually read. Section 5(2)'s alternative conditions are **not ordinarily resident OR not domiciled**, not an AND test. Work actually performed locally is treated distinctly from genuine foreign income. Section 47 requires a temporary purpose, no intent to establish residence, and less than six months' aggregate presence; six months was not silently converted to 183 days.
- The live IRD International Tax page offers a shorter worldwide-income/day-count summary which omits those statutory qualifications. The candidate explicitly preserves the Act's qualifications and the IRD's individual-assessment instruction. Registration is not equated to a universal annual filing requirement.
- Read the operative Income Tax Act amendments in the [Finance Act 2025, No. 14](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/a2025-14g.pdf), section 15, PDF pages 32-38: Board administration and registered-animal-shelter deductions. Read section 21 of the [Finance Act 2026, No. 16](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/a2026-16g.pdf), PDF pages 11-14: pension/annuity and other deduction changes. Neither replaces core sections 5 or 47. These Acts are amendment/currentness checks, not an excuse to claim every provision in the old consolidation is current.
- IRD's treaty register was read, but no country-specific treaty was summarised from its title alone.
